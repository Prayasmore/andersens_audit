## 4. DETAILED SERVICES

### 4.1 Advisory Services

We provide proactive advisory services to help organisations make informed financial and strategic decisions.

**Our advisory expertise includes:**

- Profitability and cash flow improvement strategies
- Business financing and capital structuring (equity and debt)
- Corporate finance support, including:
  - Independent Expert Reports
  - Independent Assurance Reports
- Technical accounting advisory, including:
  - Interpretation and application of accounting standards
  - Preparation of accounting manuals and policies
  - Preparation and review of financial statements
  - Forecasting and budgeting
- Business valuations for small and medium enterprises

Our advice is practical, commercially sound, and aligned with your long‑term objectives.

### 4.2 Audit & Assurance Services

Our audit methodology is risk‑focused, efficient, and aligned with the highest professional standards.

**We provide:**

- Financial statement audits for:
  - Listed entities
  - Proprietary companies
  - Charities and not‑for‑profit organisations
- Regulatory compliance audits, including support for:
  - ASIC financial services licence requirements
  - ACNC compliance obligations
  - Superannuation regulatory requirements
- Special purpose audits, including:
  - Turnover audits
  - Completion audits
  - Grant audits
- Internal control reviews and governance assessments

We utilise modern audit tools and methodologies to deliver thorough, timely, and transparent reporting that strengthens stakeholder confidence.
